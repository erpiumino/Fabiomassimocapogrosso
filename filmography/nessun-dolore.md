synopsis: >-
  Davide is a thirty-year-old man with a passion for books. He doesn’t read
  many, but he sells them from a square in Rome. His most loyal customer, Elsa,
  is also his accomplice: she supplies him with second-hand books and showers
  him with affection and tenderness.


  But something begins to unravel on an ordinary day when a child from North
  Africa is suddenly “delivered” to Davide on the street, like a package. The
  boy never leaves his side, silently following him like a faithful puppy.


  An accident leads to a desperate race to the hospital, with the young stranger
  caught between life and death. Davide is drawn into a personal hell of
  reticence, remorse, and disorientation.


  In the forbidden refuge of the emergency room, Davide meets Aminah one night.
  She has just arrived from Algeria on a migrant boat. She is a rain-soaked,
  trembling, frightened teenager, visibly pregnant and waiting to give birth.
  Davide can do nothing but offer her shelter in his home.


  But this is only the beginning. Every evening, when he returns from work,
  Davide finds more and more strangers in his apartment, once the home of a
  solitary man. He rebels in vain, but ultimately he is forced to give in,
  whether out of generosity or weakness.


  His home is no longer his alone. It becomes a home for those who have no roof
  to protect them—the many who sleep under bridges or on street corners.


  When a complaint from his neighbors lands him in prison for aiding illegal
  immigration, Davide lives his odyssey to the fullest. It is not merely a
  personal journey, but one that profoundly transforms, in one way or another,
  the feelings and lives of those around him.
